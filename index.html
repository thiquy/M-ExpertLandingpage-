Common Questions About TaaS

What is "Trust as a Service" exactly?
TaaS is our standardized framework that leverages local universities and experts to validate your technology in real ASEAN operating conditions, reducing risks and building confidence for local buyers.
How is M-Expert different from GLG, Upwork, or a consulting firm?
Unlike advisory networks, M-Expert's local experts and university partners directly manage and execute projects on the ground, delivering hands-on technical validation and implementation.
How long does a typical Living Lab validation take?
Validation periods vary by project complexity, but typically range from 3 to 6 months to ensure comprehensive testing in local conditions.
What is the cost?
Costs are structured based on the specific scope of validation and local expert involvement. We provide transparent pricing models during our initial consultation.
Which ASEAN countries are covered?
We currently focus on Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, and the Philippines, with expanding networks across the entire ASEAN region.
How are local experts vetted?
Our local experts are rigorously selected from top ASEAN universities, research institutions, and industry leaders with proven track records in technical execution and project management.
